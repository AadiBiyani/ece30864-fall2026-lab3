Pros and Cons of Agile Development 

Pros - 

1. High flexibility: users can update requirements, change priorities, and update goals very easily at the start of each cycle of a sprint
2. Continuous quality: ensures continuous testing as development continues, which can help catch bugs and systematic issues at an early stage

Cons - 

1. Low predictability: hard to predict final outcome of project inclluding cost, resources needed, etc. as project evolves through sprints
2. High Time commitment: it can take a long time to update each portion of a sprint, and daily meetings and constant collaboration demand a lot of energy and active participation from 
everybody
