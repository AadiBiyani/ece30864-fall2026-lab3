Pros and Cons of Agile Development 

Pros - 

1. High flexibility: users can update requirements, change priorities, and update goals very easily at the start of each cycle of a sprint
2. Continuous quality: ensures continuous testing as development continues, which can help catch bugs and systematic issues at an early stage

Cons - 

1. Low predictability: hard to predict final outcome of project inclluding cost, resources needed, etc. as project evolves through sprints
2. High Time commitment: it can take a long time to update each portion of a sprint, and daily meetings and constant collaboration demand a lot of energy and active participation from 
everybody

 
 Responsibilities of Scrum Master vs Product Owner

 The Product owner represents the voice of the customer and manages the direction of the product. This includes defining the vision, managing the backlog, 
 representing the stakeholders, and accepting work. 

 The Scrum master acts as the coach and facilitator for the team to ensure all goals are met. The scrum master's responsibilities includes removing
 blockers, facilitating events and meetings, coaching the team, and protecting the process of the agile methodology, ensuring it is completed.


 3 Types of Meetings during Spring Cycles 

 1. Sprint planning
  - Team decides what work needs to get done by the end of the current sprint and how to move forward with their project
  - Tickets are pulled from the backlog based on the team's capacity and speed of getting work done

2. Daily Standup 
 - Happens everyday the current sprint is running
 - Developers will update each other on their progress and any blockers they are facing and how they can move forward and get their task done

3. Backlog refinemine
 - This occurs midway through the ongoing sprint
 - developers will come together and update their tasks, story points, time needed, etc. based on the trajectory of their task in the sprint thus far
